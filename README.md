# Rapidpace CRM Demo

Reference email programmes for a crypto + fiat casino, a sportsbook and a US sweepstakes site, built for Rapidpace CRM.

Live demo: https://marcorapidpace.github.io/rapidpace-crm-demo/

Three journey pages show each programme the way a CRM manager sees it: a featured newsletter, the player journey map with waits and CRM checks, the weekly promo calendar, lifecycle triggers, segments and retention metrics.

- `index.html`: Casino (crypto + fiat), 26 emails (`casino-*`)
- `sports.html`: Sportsbook, 19 emails (`sports-*`)
- `sweeps.html`: US sweepstakes, 16 emails (`sweeps-*`), each with a plain-text version (`.txt`)

The operator, offers and legal text are fictional and for demonstration only. Templates are standalone HTML for email clients and keep the Rapidpace CRM reserved footer and subscription placeholders.
