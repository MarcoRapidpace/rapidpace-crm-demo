  • 2b. How to Deposit with Crypto — Day 3 Open
    Two paths: send crypto you already hold, or buy it with card, Apple Pay or Google Pay in the Wallet. Coins accepted, $10 minimum, typical speed.
  • 2c. Game Spotlight — Day 5 Open
    Top games using the newsletter tiles, plus the first-deposit offer. Swap the tiles to match each week's newsletter.
  • 3. Our Last Reminder — Day 7 Open
    Moved from Day 3. Honest last nudge (the package has no deadline, so no fake urgency), plus quick fixes for common deposit hiccups. Hands the player off to the newsletter.

👥 Player Value Segments

Instead of sending every offer to every depositor, players are grouped by what they deposited in the last 30 days. Each group gets offers that fit, and a cap on how many promo emails they receive. Flow emails (onboarding, deposit ladder, lifecycle triggers) are sent on top and don't count towards the cap.

Offer rule of thumb: the smallest offer that works, and recognition before bonuses for high-value players.

Control group

Hold back 10% of eligible players from each flow. Comparing their retention with players who got the emails shows the real lift the CRM creates.

Tracking tags

Every link in every email carries utm_source=rapidpace, utm_medium=email and utm_campaign=<email name>, so site analytics shows which email drove each visit and deposit.

Clicks over opens

Apple Mail pre-loads emails, which inflates open rates. Judge emails on clicks and deposits after a click instead.
