👥 Player Value Segments

Instead of sending every offer to every depositor, players are grouped by what they deposited in the last 30 days. Each group gets offers that fit, and a cap on how many promo emails they receive. Flow emails (onboarding, deposit ladder, lifecycle triggers) are sent on top and don't count towards the cap.

SegmentWhoWhat they getPromo emails / week
🆕 NewRegistered or first deposit in the last 7 daysOnboarding and deposit-ladder flows only. No weekly promos yet, so the welcome package gets full attention.0
🥉 Low valueUnder $100 deposited in the last 30 daysNewsletter, slots cashback and free-spin offers. No large deposit matches.2
🥈 Mid value$100 – $999 in the last 30 daysFull weekly calendar: cashback, Free Bet Club, ACCA boosts, live happy hour, newsletter.3
🥇 High value$1,000+ in the last 30 days (roughly Bronze rank and up)VIP Reload Tuesday and VIP messages, fewer mass promos, personal follow-up from the VIP team.2 + personal
💤 DormantNo deposit or login in 30+ daysWin-back emails only (D+7, D+14, D+30).1

Thresholds are a starting point: review them after 4 weeks of data. More segments can be layered on top, for example slots vs live casino players, bonus-sensitive vs no-bonus players, or by region. Offer rule of thumb: the smallest offer that works, and recognition before bonuses for high-value players.

Control group

Hold back 10% of eligible players from each flow. Comparing their retention with players who got the emails shows the real lift the CRM creates.

Tracking tags

Every link in every email carries utm_source=rapidpace, utm_medium=email and utm_campaign=<email name>, so site analytics shows which email drove each visit and deposit.

Clicks over opens

Apple Mail pre-loads emails, which inflates open rates. Judge emails on clicks and deposits after a click instead.
