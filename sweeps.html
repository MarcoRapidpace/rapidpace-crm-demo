🇺🇸 Why sweeps CRM is different

Rapidpace US runs on the dual-currency sweepstakes model: players never deposit or cash out. They play with free or purchased Gold Coins, and with Sweeps Coins that are always free and can be redeemed for prizes. That changes the words, the offers, the data and the rules of every email.

Two coins, not cash

Gold Coins are for fun; Sweeps Coins are the sweepstakes entry and are always free. Emails sell Gold Coin packages and never SC.

No purchase necessary

Every promo email carries the NPN block, links the Sweepstakes Rules and mentions free routes (daily login, mail-in).

Different vocabulary

Purchase, play, redeem, prizes. Never deposit, bet, wager, cash out, withdraw or gamble.

21+ and state-gated

15 excluded states (Oklahoma's ban takes effect 1 Nov 2026) and more bills expected in 2027. Suppress by residence state on every send, so new bans are covered.

No loss-chasing

The Responsible Gambling page promises no promotions that reward chasing a loss, so no loss-based cashback or "win it back" copy.

KYC before redemption

Light check at sign-up, full KYC before the first redemption. "Verify early" nudges are a core CRM job.

Taxes and service notices

$2,000/yr triggers W-9 + 1099-MISC; 11-month dormancy and state-exit emails are required by the site's own terms.

Free players matter

Most players never buy. Daily-login habit and referrals are the growth engine; purchases follow engagement.

🎁 Flow 1 · Activation
  • 1. Welcome to Rapidpace US — Day 0
    Sent on sign-up. Welcome coins (50,000 GC + 1 SC, credited at sign-up), the two coins explained, free daily coins, and a soft mention of the First Purchase Bonus.
    Subject: Welcome to Rapidpace US 🦁
  • 2. Your Free Daily Coins — Day 1
    Builds the daily-login habit. The four free ways to collect SC (daily login, Daily Spin, Facebook giveaways, mail-in) and the Top 10.
    Subject: Your free daily coins are ready
  • 3. How Prizes Work — Day 3
    Sweeps Coins 101: collect → play → verify → redeem. Pushes early identity verification, the main cause of delayed first redemptions.
    Subject: How Sweeps Coins and prizes work
  • 4. First Purchase Bonus — Day 5
    The three welcome packages ($10 / $25 / $50, choose one). Clear that SC are free, a purchase does not improve chances, and limits are available.
    Subject: A welcome boost for your first package
  • 5. Welcome Recap — Day 7
    Honest last onboarding email: everything waiting in the account, no fake deadline. Hands the player off to the Weekly.
    Subject: Everything waiting in your account
💳 Flow 2 · Purchase & Redemption
  • 6. Thanks for Your First Purchase — +1h
    Complements the platform receipt: how to play SC, 1x play-through, verify now, purchase limits.
    Subject: Thanks for your first purchase
  • 7. Verify to Redeem — triggerService
    For players with a redeemable balance above the minimum who are not yet verified. Document checklist from the Customer Acceptance Policy. No promotional content.
    Subject: One step before your first redemption
  • 8. First Redemption — 24h after payout
    Complements the platform payout email: timings, the $2,000 W-9 / 1099-MISC rule, VIP teaser.
    Subject: Congrats on your first redemption
👑 Flow 3 · VIP
  • 9. New VIP Level
    Triggered on level change. Level-up SC and Coinback by level (Bronze → Pinnacle), VIP host.
    Subject: You've reached a new VIP level
  • 10. Weekly Coinback
    Triggered when Coinback is credited (Wednesdays 9:00 AM PT). Framed on play and VIP level, never on losses.
    Subject: Your weekly Coinback is in
📰 Weekly & Referral
  • 11. The Rapidpace US Weekly
    Thursday roundup: Top 10, free-coins checklist (incl. mail-in), quick-play spotlight, promo calendar. Swap games each issue.
    Subject: The Rapidpace US Weekly: top games and free coins
  • 12. Refer & Earn
    Monthly to active players. The 5 steps from the promotions page and referral rate by VIP level.
    Subject: Share Rapidpace US, get rewarded
⏳ Win-Back & Compliance
  • 13. Daily Coins Waiting — 3 days quiet
    Light nudge for active players: free daily coins and trending games, no purchase offer.
    Subject: Your daily coins are waiting
  • 14. Daily Coins Still Here — 30 days lapsed
    Catch-up email. Reminds players that redeemable SC do not expire. Optional small free SC gift, RG-gated.
    Subject: Your free daily coins are still here
  • 15. Keep Your Account Active — 11 monthsService
    Required by the General Terms ("we will email you before applying any inactivity measure").
    Subject: Keep your Rapidpace US account active
  • 16. State Eligibility UpdateService
    Required by the Customer Acceptance Policy when a state becomes ineligible. Dated close-down and redemption window, no promotional content.
    Subject: Important update about your Rapidpace US account
